## **Figma**  April - June 2026
### **IceBreaker App concept**
  An app designed to help people break the ice and start conversations at social gatherings. Users create and personalize their own profiles by adding their interests, hobbies, and other information they are comfortable sharing.

When a group gathers, users can join a Kahoot-style interactive game together. The game generates questions and prompts based on the participants' profiles and interests. Players answer questions individually, and the results can reveal shared interests, fun differences, and potential conversation topics.

The goal is not simply to win the game, but to give people natural conversation starters and help them get to know one another in a fun and low-pressure way.
